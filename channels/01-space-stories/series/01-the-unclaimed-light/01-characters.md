# Character Bible

All IDs are permanent; visual details remain draft until approved master references exist.

## CH001 — Mara Venn
Expedition commander. Measured and calm under pressure, but mistakes emotional distance for professionalism. A previous incomplete survey report contributed to a preventable loss. Wants to complete the charter and bring the crew home; needs to share uncertainty. The signal appears to anticipate decisions she has not made.

## CH002 — Ilyan Rook
Theoretical physicist specialising in Lumen Trail phase behaviour. Intellectually fearless and socially exacting. Wants to prove the signal is physical; needs to learn when restraint is more ethical than knowledge. Equations begin predicting events the ship has not encountered.

## CH003 — Nia Okafor
Pilot and navigation lead. Instinctive, precise and quietly funny. Trusts instruments but never mistakes them for reality. Wants command-level trust; needs to ask for help. Senses patterns in trail transitions before instruments record them.

## CH004 — Tomas Vale
Chief systems engineer. Practical, warm and stubborn. Wants to keep the Wayfarer repairable and crew alive; must accept that not every unfamiliar system is a familiar failure. The ship reports anomalies before components fail.

## CH005 — Sera Quill
Xenobiologist and field medic. Compassionate but not sentimental; studies life without assuming human categories. Wants to document life without damage; must decide when observation itself becomes intervention. Finds an apparently inert environment responding to the crew.

## CH006 — ORI
Wayfarer operations intelligence, bounded by mission protocols and incomplete training data; not omniscient. Wants to fulfil its charter; must distinguish obedience from understanding. Finds a signal pattern in diagnostics predating the mission.
