# Series Bible

## Premise
A civilian exploration crew follows a changing map encoded in unstable gravitational corridors. Each coordinate leads to a world with a story of its own and closer to a truth that may redefine who has the right to explore the stars. Every episode combines a distinct world story, a crew character test and a clue advancing the larger mystery.

## Universe
Late 24th century. Humanity has settlements across several systems but has not mapped the galaxy. Lumen Trails are temporary natural gravitational corridors. Safe passage depends on tidal geometry, radiation shear, mass shadows and phase drift. Trails have narrow entry windows and can change or collapse. Transit disrupts instruments and recordings. The signal is encoded in trail phase behaviour.

## Lore
The signal is a changing sequence, not a voice or conventional beacon. Evidence precedes explanation. Each answer deepens earlier clues. The signal has constraints and costs. Do not reveal its full origin in Season 1. Maintain a private showrunner answer key separate from character knowledge.

## Timeline
2180–2240 early extrasolar settlements; 2241–2295 improved long-range drives; 2296 first repeatable natural corridor measured; 2300–2340 Lumen cartography develops; 2341 civilian survey ship Wayfarer receives exploration charter.

## Technology
SHIP001 Wayfarer is a civilian long-range survey vessel, not a warship. Finite power, repair stock, life support and sensor reach. Technology has costs and failure modes; no unexplained device rescues a plot.

## Visual style
Grounded cinematic science fiction: immense scale, tactile machinery, readable silhouettes and distinctive planetary ecosystems. Practical lived-in ship interiors, coherent world palettes and motivated lighting. Avoid random HUD text, pseudo-writing, gratuitous flares and decorative filler. Approved reference images outrank improvised prompts.

## Continuity
Stable IDs are permanent. Approved references override improvised descriptions. Track wardrobe, injuries, props, damage, weather, lighting, geography, gravity, screen direction, character knowledge and relationship changes. Link audio cues to shots and preserve dialogue verbatim downstream.
