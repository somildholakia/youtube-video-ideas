# Season 1 Location Reference Guide

Generate one separate ChatGPT reference image per major location. No text, labels, maps, UI or signage. Attach the approved image to all corresponding shot prompts.

- LOC001 Kestrel Null: apparently empty system, dim star, airless dark planet, precise mineral dust ring and vast negative space.
- LOC002 Lumen Trail: temporary gravitational corridor, physically plausible lensing and parallel star distortions; no magical portal rim.
- LOC003 Folded Corridor: overlapping gravity lens shaped like a shifting hinge; geometry responds to ship mass.
- LOC004 Quiet Pocket: calm starfield beyond the folded corridor; lens appears to open inward.
- LOC005 Garden World: translucent leaves, root bridges, luminous botanical ecosystem and mineral sub-roots; no plant faces.
- LOC006 Relay Debris Lane: broken communication satellites and metallic fragments in hard vacuum.
- LOC007 Veyra: remote habitat on a barren moon, pressure domes, clear landing boundary lights and lived-in community interior.
- LOC008 Exchange Station: abandoned mineral-and-ceramic station, shifting glyph surfaces and non-verbal architecture.
- LOC009 Orison Ice Moon: white ice crust glowing from beneath, subglacial tunnels and fragile transparent microorganisms.
- LOC010 Dark Expanse: sparse starfield, one navigation buoy and minimal visual landmarks.
- LOC011 Peregrine: aged civilian survey ship related in geometry to the Wayfarer, oxidised hull and silent crew spaces.
- LOC012 Return Corridor: smooth gravitational route with a visible anchor boundary and no conventional powered machinery.
- LOC013 Beacon Field: dark unlit beacon surrounded by mineral filaments and a distributed living network, not a central machine core.
- LOC014 Archive Moon: airless dead moon, buried chambers and mineral seams aligning from multiple viewing positions.
- LOC015 Terminal Trail: narrowing Lumen Trail, collapsing rear boundary and unstable reference stars.
- LOC016 Unrecorded Region: unfamiliar dense starfield with large-scale arcs absent from all known maps; no visible city or creature.
