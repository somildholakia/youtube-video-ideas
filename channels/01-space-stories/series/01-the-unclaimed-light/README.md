# The Unclaimed Light

Original science-fiction exploration drama for a mature general audience. Nine planned seasons, 14 episodes each, 40–45 minutes per episode (42:40 production assembly target).

Humanity maps unstable gravitational corridors called Lumen Trails. A civilian exploration ship discovers a signal embedded in a seemingly empty star system. The signal changes after every jump and reveals new coordinates. It is not a distress call. It is a map—and its next destination appears to anticipate the crew's decisions.

Each destination has its own ecology, history, conflict and consequences. All characters, names, designs, lore, dialogue and stories are original.
