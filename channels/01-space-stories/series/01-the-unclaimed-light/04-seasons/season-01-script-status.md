## Important production distinction
The current screenplay files are timed visual production drafts: they establish each sequence, beat, key dialogue exchange, continuity and visual action. They are not yet dialogue-dense, actor-ready 40–45-minute shooting scripts. Their generated sound beds and visual holds must be conformed to actual audio. Expand dialogue and scene blocking before recording a fully dialogue-led episode.

## Remaining creator-side work
Character and location reference artwork must be generated and approved in ChatGPT. Then generate all collage images, inspect the exact panel count and consistency, generate voice/audio, edit and review each final episode. No images, voices or finished videos are claimed as already generated.
