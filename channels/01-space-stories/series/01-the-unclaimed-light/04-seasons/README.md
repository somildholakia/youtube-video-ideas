# Nine-Season Roadmap

1. The First Coordinate — changing map and first destination sequence.
2. The Quiet Constellation — systems omitted from known charts.
3. The Archive Beneath — a distributed record remembers history differently.
4. The Listening Dark — a phenomenon responds to observation and language.
5. The Broken Meridian — map fractures and rival expeditions disagree.
6. The Cartographers' War — struggle over access and knowledge.
7. The Unwritten Region — region absent from navigational models.
8. The Origin Equation — network origin, limits and costs.
9. The Last Coordinate — map purpose and the crew's final choice.

Each season initially has 14 episodes. Expand only if demand and story quality justify it. Episode guides are roadmaps, not completed screenplays.
