# Season 01 — The First Coordinate

Status: Story roadmap. Each episode combines a distinct world problem, a crew character turn and a clue advancing the season mystery. Concepts may be revised in script development; these are not completed screenplays.

## S01E01 — The Empty System
A blank system contains a signal that changes after the first jump.

## S01E02 — A Door Made of Gravity
Nia chooses between retreat and an uncharted exit.

## S01E03 — The Garden That Remembers
An ecosystem responds to patterns the crew leaves behind.

## S01E04 — Three Minutes of Tomorrow
ORI records a ship event before it happens, incompletely.

## S01E05 — The Quiet Settlement
A remote habitat refuses contact for reasons that challenge assumptions.

## S01E06 — The Weight of Names
A translation problem turns first contact into an ethical test.

## S01E07 — The Signal in the Ice
A frozen moon preserves a trace of a forgotten route.

## S01E08 — A Map with Missing Edges
The signal omits destinations when the crew acts predictably.

## S01E09 — The Other Survey Ship
A derelict carries the Wayfarer's registration.

## S01E10 — The Cost of Returning
A return corridor demands a sacrifice.

## S01E11 — The Unlit Beacon
A beacon is powered by a living process.

## S01E12 — The Shape of an Answer
The first coordinate pattern is assembled and found incomplete.

## S01E13 — Where the Trail Ends
A route collapses behind the ship.

## S01E14 — The First Coordinate
The signal addresses Mara by a choice she has not made.
