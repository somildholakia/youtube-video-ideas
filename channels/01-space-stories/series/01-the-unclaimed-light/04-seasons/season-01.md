# Season 01 — The First Coordinate

**Status:** Season roadmap plus timed visual production drafts and shot/audio manifests for all 14 episodes. The episode files are not dialogue-dense final shooting scripts; expand and rehearse dialogue, then verify rendered runtime before voice recording and export.

## Season arc
A changing signal is embedded in unstable gravitational corridors. The crew initially reads it as a route, then learns it responds to choices, records consequences and may be connected to earlier expeditions. Season 1 ends with a decision marker associated with Mara, without revealing the signal's origin.

## Episodes

1. **S01E01 — The Empty System:** A blank system contains a signal that changes after the first jump.
2. **S01E02 — A Door Made of Gravity:** Nia chooses between retreat and an uncharted exit.
3. **S01E03 — The Garden That Remembers:** An ecosystem responds to patterns the crew leaves behind.
4. **S01E04 — Three Minutes of Tomorrow:** ORI records ship events before they happen, incompletely.
5. **S01E05 — The Quiet Settlement:** A remote habitat refuses contact until the crew respect its boundary.
6. **S01E06 — The Weight of Names:** Translation turns first contact into an ethical test.
7. **S01E07 — The Signal in the Ice:** A frozen moon preserves a trace of a forgotten route.
8. **S01E08 — A Map with Missing Edges:** The signal changes when the crew choose predictable routes.
9. **S01E09 — The Other Survey Ship:** A derelict carries the Wayfarer's registration and an older mission log.
10. **S01E10 — The Cost of Returning:** A return corridor requires a freely chosen memory anchor.
11. **S01E11 — The Unlit Beacon:** A living network powers a navigation beacon.
12. **S01E12 — The Shape of an Answer:** The assembled map is a boundary around an unknown region.
13. **S01E13 — Where the Trail Ends:** The crew agree to observe the threshold without crossing it.
14. **S01E14 — The First Coordinate:** The signal associates Mara with a choice she has not made; another unlit point appears.
