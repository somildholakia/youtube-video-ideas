# Season 03 — The Archive Beneath

Status: Story roadmap. Each episode combines a distinct world problem, a crew character turn and a clue advancing the season mystery. Concepts may be revised in script development; these are not completed screenplays.

## S03E01 — Below the Basalt
A buried structure stores impressions of visitors.

## S03E02 — The Indexers
An archive species classifies questions, not answers.

## S03E03 — A Memory with No Owner
Two characters remember an event differently.

## S03E04 — The Unfinished Catalogue
A record ends when the crew arrives.

## S03E05 — Borrowed Histories
A settlement's founding story is a shared memory.

## S03E06 — The Room of First Things
Objects from worlds not yet visited appear.

## S03E07 — The Archivist's Error
One corrupted entry changes a civilisation's history.

## S03E08 — A Thousand Versions
The archive contains mutually exclusive histories.

## S03E09 — The Names We Leave
Mara confronts harm caused by incomplete records.

## S03E10 — The Living Index
The archive is partly biological.

## S03E11 — The Last Footnote
A marginal note identifies a missing route.

## S03E12 — The Memory Tax
Archive access has an ecological cost.

## S03E13 — What Was Not Written
The crew discovers an intentional absence.

## S03E14 — The Archive Beneath
The map was designed to be questioned.
