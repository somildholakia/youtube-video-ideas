# Worlds, Ship and Technology

Register important locations with permanent LOC### IDs. Record gravity, atmosphere, temperature, sky, light, geography, hazards, ecology, soundscape and approved references. Every world needs a distinctive ecological thesis. Unclaimed by humans does not mean empty or available.

## SHIP001 — Wayfarer
Draft canon. Civilian long-range survey vessel, not combat craft. Long central spine, forward sensor assembly and offset habitation/engineering modules. Final geometry requires approved reference artwork. Capabilities: survey, limited repair, sampling and Lumen Trail measurement. Limits: finite power/supplies, vulnerable sensors, limited medical capacity, no dedicated heavy weapons.
